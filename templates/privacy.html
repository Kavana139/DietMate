Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, password (stored as a secure hash)
  • Health data: Age, gender, height, weight, dietary goals
  • Usage data: Meals logged, calorie intake, streak data
  • Security logs: Login times, IP addresses, device type

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate personalised nutrition targets
  • To generate meal plans matching your goals
  • To send security notifications (login alerts, password resets)
  • To track your progress over time

3. Data Security

Your password is never stored in plain text — it is hashed using bcrypt with a work factor of 12. Health data is stored in a secured database accessible only to your account.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. The only external services used are:

  • Your email provider (for sending verification and notification emails)
  • Nutrition APIs (for food data lookup — no personal data is shared)

5. Your Rights

  • Access your data at any time from the Account page
  • Update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data

6. Cookies

DietMate uses only a session cookie for authentication and a localStorage value for theme preference. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

7. Contact

For privacy concerns or data deletion requests, contact us through the app.
